Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Overview

Rclone Google Drive Authentication provides OAuth 2.0 authorization for connecting the open-source Rclone application to Google Drive.

Information Accessed

The application requests Google Drive permissions necessary for the authorized file operations. Access is limited to the OAuth scopes approved by the user.

Depending on those permissions and the user's Rclone configuration, operations may include listing, reading, creating, updating, and deleting authorized files.

Use of Information

Google Drive data is accessed only to perform file operations initiated or configured by the authorized user.

The integration does not use Google Drive data for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

Authentication and Credentials

Authentication uses Google's OAuth 2.0 authorization system. OAuth credentials and tokens are managed by Rclone in its configured environment.

Users are responsible for protecting their Rclone configuration and associated authentication credentials.

Data Storage and Sharing

Files are processed and transferred according to the user's Rclone configuration. The integration does not operate a separate hosted service for storing synchronized files.

Data stored in Google Drive remains subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

Data Retention and Deletion

File retention and deletion are determined by the user's Rclone operations, synchronization settings, and the storage services involved.

Revoking authorization prevents further access through the revoked credentials but does not automatically delete previously transferred files.

User Control

Users may review or revoke this application's access through their Google Account connections .

Website Privacy

This informational website does not request Google account credentials, receive OAuth tokens, or directly access Google Drive files.

The website does not include application-operated analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts. Its hosting provider may process standard connection information according to its own privacy policy.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application operator using the support email provided on the Google OAuth consent screen.
